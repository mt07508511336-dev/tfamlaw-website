Shepherd and Wedderburn Launches Family Law Practice to Enhance Client Services

13 November 2026

At Thompson Family Law, we understand the importance of staying informed about developments in the legal community that may impact our clients. Recently, Shepherd and Wedderburn, a prominent Scottish law firm, announced the launch of their dedicated family law practice. This expansion reflects a growing recognition of the need for specialized legal support in family matters.

The new team is led by partners Alison Edmondson and Robert Gilmour, both seasoned professionals in family law. They bring with them a group of five additional lawyers, enhancing the firm's capacity to handle complex family-related cases. Ms. Edmondson specializes in financial issues arising from separation and divorce, particularly those involving businesses, pensions, and trusts. Mr. Gilmour offers expertise across the full spectrum of family law, including assisted reproduction and surrogacy matters.

This development is significant for individuals seeking legal assistance in family matters. The establishment of a dedicated family law practice within a well-established firm like Shepherd and Wedderburn indicates a commitment to providing comprehensive and specialized support. Clients can expect tailored advice that considers the nuances of family dynamics and the complexities of the law.

For those navigating family-related legal challenges, it's crucial to have access to experienced professionals who can offer clear guidance and compassionate support. The expansion of family law services within established firms provides more options for individuals to find representation that aligns with their specific needs.

At Thompson Family Law, we commend initiatives that enhance the availability and quality of family law services. We remain dedicated to offering personalized and practical legal support to our clients, ensuring they receive the guidance necessary to navigate their unique situations effectively.
