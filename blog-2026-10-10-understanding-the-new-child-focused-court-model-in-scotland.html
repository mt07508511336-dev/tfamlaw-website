Understanding the New Child-Focused Court Model in Scotland

10 October 2026

Recent developments in Scottish family law have introduced the Child-Focused Court (CFC) model, aiming to make family court proceedings less adversarial and more centered on the child's well-being. This approach seeks to reduce conflict between parents and prioritize the experiences and needs of children involved in private law disputes.

The CFC model, initially launched as Pathfinder in 2022 and rebranded earlier this year, emphasizes early information gathering and a less confrontational process. However, it's crucial to understand that while the procedures have evolved, the fundamental legal responsibilities of the Family Court remain unchanged. This was underscored in a recent Court of Appeal case, where the court clarified that the new model does not diminish the court's duties, especially in cases involving allegations of domestic abuse. The court stated that the CFC model "has not altered the substantive legal framework" of the family court. [Source: Today's Family Lawyer]

For families navigating the complexities of separation or divorce, this shift offers a more compassionate and child-centered approach. It encourages parents to focus on cooperative solutions that serve the best interests of their children, rather than engaging in contentious legal battles.
