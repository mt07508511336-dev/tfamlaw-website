MPs Warn of Rising Miscarriages of Justice Due to Legal Aid Decline

3 October 2026

At Thompson Family Law, we are deeply concerned about the recent findings from the Justice Committee's report, which highlights a significant decline in legal aid availability across the UK. This reduction poses a serious threat to the fairness and efficiency of our justice system.

The report points out that both criminal and civil legal aid have not kept pace with the rising cost of living. As a result, many individuals who would have previously qualified for assistance are now left without support. This situation has led to an increase in self-representation, where individuals navigate complex legal proceedings without professional guidance. Such circumstances elevate the risk of miscarriages of justice and place additional strain on our court system.

In response to these challenges, the government has proposed an investment of up to £34 million annually to bolster criminal legal aid. This funding aims to enhance the sustainability of the legal sector and expedite the resolution of cases, thereby reducing delays for victims. While this initiative is a positive step, it is crucial that these funds are allocated effectively to address the root causes of the current crisis.

At Thompson Family Law, we believe that access to justice is a fundamental right. The current state of legal aid threatens this principle, leaving many without the necessary support to navigate legal challenges. We urge the government to implement comprehensive reforms that ensure legal aid is accessible, sustainable, and capable of meeting the needs of those who rely on it most.

For individuals facing legal issues, it's essential to seek professional advice. Our team is committed to providing guidance and support to help you understand your rights and options. If you have concerns about how these developments may affect you, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
