Scottish Government Launches Independent Advocacy Service for Families Bereaved by Deaths in Custody

19 November 2026

The Scottish Government has recently introduced an independent advocacy service designed to support families who have lost loved ones in custody. This initiative aims to provide these families with the necessary assistance to navigate the complex processes that follow such tragic events.

When a death occurs in custody, families often find themselves facing a maze of legal procedures, inquiries, and investigations. The emotional toll is immense, and understanding the steps involved can be overwhelming. Recognizing this, the new advocacy service offers guidance and support to help families through these challenging times.

Key aspects of the service include:

  • Information and Guidance: Providing clear explanations about the processes that follow a death in custody, including investigations and potential legal proceedings.
  • Emotional Support: Offering a compassionate ear and connecting families with counseling services to help them cope with their loss.
  • Representation: Assisting families in communicating with authorities and ensuring their voices are heard during inquiries.

This initiative underscores the government's commitment to enhancing access to justice and ensuring that families affected by deaths in custody receive the support they need. It's a significant step towards acknowledging the unique challenges these families face and providing them with tailored assistance.

At Thompson Family Law, we understand the profound impact such events can have on families. While we hope no one has to endure such a loss, it's reassuring to know that resources like this advocacy service are available to provide support during the most difficult times.
