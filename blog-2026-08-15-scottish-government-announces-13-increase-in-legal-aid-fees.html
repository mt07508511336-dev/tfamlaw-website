Scottish Government Announces 13% Increase in Legal Aid Fees for Solicitors

15 August 2026

The Scottish Government has recently announced a significant development in the legal aid sector: a 13% increase in legal aid fees for solicitors, set to take effect on September 1, 2026. This decision follows productive negotiations with the legal profession, represented by the Law Society of Scotland and the Scottish Solicitors Bar Association.

For context, legal aid is a system designed to ensure that individuals who cannot afford legal representation can still access justice. Solicitors who provide these services are compensated through legal aid fees. Over the years, there have been concerns about the adequacy of these fees, with many in the profession advocating for adjustments to reflect the complexities and demands of modern legal work.

The forthcoming increase means that the core fixed payment for criminal cases will rise from £572 to £646.36. This adjustment is part of a broader package of reforms aimed at supporting the legal profession and enhancing the delivery of legal services across Scotland.

In addition to the fee increase, the Scottish Government has committed to funding up to 40 legal aid traineeship places from the summer of 2026. This initiative is designed to attract new talent to the field and ensure the sustainability of legal aid services in the future. Furthermore, there will be digital support funding available to help firms equip trainees with the necessary tools to perform their roles effectively.

These measures are part of a concerted effort to create a legal assistance system that is fit for the 21st century. The establishment of an Independent Fee Review Mechanism Group underscores this commitment, aiming to develop a more transparent, evidence-based, and sustainable approach to reviewing legal aid fees.

At Thompson Family Law, we welcome these developments. As a family-run firm with offices in Glasgow (Bishopbriggs), Edinburgh, Coatbridge, and Airdrie, we understand the importance of accessible legal services. The increase in legal aid fees and the support for traineeships will undoubtedly strengthen the legal profession, ensuring that individuals and families across Scotland continue to receive the quality legal assistance they deserve.

For those seeking legal aid services, it's essential to stay informed about these changes and understand how they might impact your access to legal representation. If you have any questions or need assistance, our team at Thompson Family Law is here to help.
