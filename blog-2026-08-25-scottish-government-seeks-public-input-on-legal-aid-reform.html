Scottish Government Seeks Public Input on Legal Aid Reform

25 August 2026

The Scottish Government has recently launched a consultation to gather public opinions on proposed reforms to the legal aid system. This initiative aims to modernize the system, making it more efficient and accessible for both those who rely on legal aid and the professionals who provide these essential services.

Legal aid is a cornerstone of our justice system, offering financial assistance to individuals who might otherwise be unable to afford legal representation. It ensures that everyone, regardless of their financial situation, has the opportunity to pursue or defend their legal rights. In Scotland, the legal aid system supports approximately 170,000 grants each year, with total expenditure reaching £169 million in 2024-25—the highest annual spend on legal aid in the country's history.

The proposed reforms focus on several key areas:

  • Simplifying Processes: By reducing duplication and streamlining procedures, the aim is to expedite decision-making, allowing cases to progress more swiftly through the system.
  • Enhancing Access: Improvements are planned to make it easier for individuals to access legal aid services, ensuring that those in need can obtain support without unnecessary barriers.
  • Empowering Solicitors: The reforms seek to provide greater autonomy for accredited solicitors, enabling them to deliver services more effectively and respond to clients' needs with greater flexibility.

These changes are designed to create a more user-friendly and efficient legal aid system that better serves the people of Scotland. By involving the public in this consultation, the government aims to ensure that the reforms reflect the needs and experiences of those who use and provide legal aid services.

At Thompson Family Law, we understand the vital role that legal aid plays in ensuring access to justice. We encourage our clients and colleagues to participate in this consultation, sharing their insights and experiences to help shape a legal aid system that is fair, efficient, and responsive to the needs of all Scots.

For more information on the consultation and how to contribute your views, please visit the Scottish Government's official website.
