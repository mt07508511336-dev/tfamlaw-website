Scottish Legal Aid Board Advocates for Transformational Reform in Legal Aid System

19 October 2026

Access to justice is a cornerstone of a fair society, ensuring that everyone, regardless of their financial situation, can seek legal assistance when needed. Recognizing the evolving needs of Scotland's populace, the Scottish Legal Aid Board (SLAB) has recently outlined a vision for a transformed legal aid system. This initiative aims to place users at the heart of the system, ensuring it remains responsive and effective.

In response to the Scottish Government's consultation on legal aid reform, SLAB has proposed a comprehensive approach that emphasizes operational independence while enhancing access to justice. Their vision includes collaborating closely with stakeholders to develop strategic plans that align with public policy objectives, such as reducing poverty and advancing equality. By focusing on these areas, SLAB aims to ensure that individuals can protect their rights, resolve legal issues, and settle disputes more efficiently.

One of the key aspects of SLAB's proposal is the commitment to maintaining operational independence from Ministers, regardless of the government of the day. This independence is crucial to ensure that the legal aid system operates without political influence, focusing solely on the needs of the users and the principles of justice.

Furthermore, SLAB's vision includes working closely with stakeholders to develop strategic plans that deliver agreed public policy objectives. This collaborative approach aims to support actions that reduce poverty, advance equality, and provide access to agreed levels of help on priority issues. By aligning the legal aid system with broader societal goals, SLAB seeks to create a more holistic and effective framework for legal assistance.

At Thompson Family Law, we understand the importance of these proposed reforms. As a family-run firm with offices in Glasgow (Bishopbriggs), Edinburgh, Coatbridge, and Airdrie, we are committed to providing accessible and effective legal assistance to our communities. We believe that a modernized legal aid system, as envisioned by SLAB, will significantly enhance our ability to serve clients who rely on legal aid for family law matters, civil disputes, and other legal challenges.

We encourage our clients and community members to stay informed about these developments. Understanding the proposed changes and how they may impact access to legal services is essential. As always, Thompson Family Law remains dedicated to guiding you through any legal challenges you may face, ensuring you receive the support and representation you deserve.
