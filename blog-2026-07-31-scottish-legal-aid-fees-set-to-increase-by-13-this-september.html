Scottish Legal Aid Fees Set to Increase by 13% This September

31 July 2026

We have some encouraging news for both legal professionals and those seeking legal assistance in Scotland. Starting this September, solicitors providing legal aid will receive a 13% increase in fees. This adjustment is part of a broader initiative aimed at strengthening the legal aid system and ensuring that quality legal support remains accessible to all.

Beyond the fee increase, the Scottish Government is introducing additional measures to support the legal profession. These include funding for up to 40 Legal Aid Traineeship positions and financial assistance to help law firms invest in digital tools. This support is designed to enhance the efficiency of legal services and ensure that solicitors are well-equipped to meet the needs of their clients.

Since 2019, cumulative fee increases have surpassed 25%, outpacing inflation. The most recent rise in April 2023 brought total additional funding to £31 million since 2019. This consistent investment underscores a commitment to maintaining a robust and sustainable legal aid system.

At Thompson Family Law, we welcome these developments. We believe that fair compensation for legal professionals is essential to attract and retain talent within the sector. Moreover, by investing in the future of legal aid through traineeships and technology, we can continue to provide high-quality legal services to those who need them most.

For individuals seeking legal assistance, these changes mean that the legal aid system is being strengthened to better serve your needs. Whether you're dealing with family matters, civil disputes, or require legal aid, rest assured that support is available and continually improving.

As always, if you have any questions or need guidance on legal matters, our team at Thompson Family Law is here to help. We are committed to providing compassionate and practical legal support to our community.
