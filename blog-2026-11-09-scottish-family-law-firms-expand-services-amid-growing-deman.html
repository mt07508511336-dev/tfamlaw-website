Scottish Family Law Firms Expand Services Amid Growing Demand

9 November 2026

In recent developments within Scotland's legal sector, two prominent law firms have significantly expanded their family law services, reflecting the growing demand for specialized legal support in this area.

Shepherd and Wedderburn, a well-established firm, has launched a dedicated Family Law practice. This new division is led by partners Alison Edmondson and Robert Gilmour, both recognized for their expertise in complex family law matters. They are supported by a team of five additional lawyers, enhancing the firm's capacity to handle cases involving financial provisions on divorce, child law disputes, mediation, arbitration, and issues related to assisted reproduction and surrogacy. This expansion allows clients to access comprehensive family law advice alongside other legal services offered by the firm. [Source: Shepherd and Wedderburn]

Similarly, Burges Salmon has introduced a specialist family law team in Edinburgh, spearheaded by partner Rachael Kelsey. Joining her are senior associate Nina Derrin, solicitor Annabel Blackwood, trainee solicitor Iona Barrie, and executive assistant Lorraine Murphy. The team is set to grow further with the addition of director John West in February 2027. This strategic move aims to bolster the firm's ability to support clients in complex, high-value, and international family law matters involving Scots law. [Source: Scottish Legal News]

These expansions underscore a notable trend in the Scottish legal landscape: an increasing recognition of the complexities and sensitivities inherent in family law cases. As families navigate challenging situations such as divorce, child custody disputes, and financial settlements, the need for specialized legal guidance becomes paramount.
