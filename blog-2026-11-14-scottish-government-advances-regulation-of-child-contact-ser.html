Scottish Government Advances Regulation of Child Contact Services

14 November 2026

Ensuring the safety and well-being of children during family transitions is paramount. Recognizing this, the Scottish Government has announced that, starting April 2027, child contact services will come under the regulation of the Care Inspectorate. This move aims to standardize and enhance the quality of services that facilitate contact between children and non-resident parents in a safe, neutral, and child-friendly environment.

Child contact services play a crucial role in the family justice system, especially in situations where direct contact between a child and a parent requires supervision or support. By bringing these services under the Care Inspectorate's remit, the government seeks to strengthen safeguards and promote consistent, trauma-informed practices across the board.

The Care Inspectorate is currently in a preparatory phase, working closely with child contact services and stakeholders to develop a Quality Improvement Framework. This framework will guide services in adopting best practices and ensuring that children's needs remain at the forefront. Regular inspections will be conducted to monitor compliance and drive continuous improvement.

In tandem with these regulatory efforts, Relationships Scotland has been developing a national training framework for child contact practitioners. Supported in part by the Scottish Government, this initiative focuses on essential areas such as understanding domestic abuse, implementing trauma-informed practices, and managing conflict effectively. Such training is vital to equip practitioners with the skills necessary to support families during challenging times.

At Thompson Family Law, we welcome these developments as a positive step toward enhancing the quality and safety of child contact services in Scotland. For families navigating the complexities of separation and ensuring meaningful relationships between children and both parents, these regulations offer reassurance that services will adhere to high standards of care and professionalism.
