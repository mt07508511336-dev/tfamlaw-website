Scottish Courts Publish Latest Civil Case Statistics: Key Insights

17 November 2026

The Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service has recently released updated statistics on civil cases across Scotland, covering the period from 2022/23 to the present financial year. These figures offer valuable insights into the types of cases being brought before our courts and the prevailing legal trends.

One notable finding is that debt-related actions constitute approximately 27.3% of all civil cases. This indicates that financial disputes remain a significant concern for many individuals and businesses. If you're facing challenges with debt recovery or disputes, it's crucial to seek legal advice promptly to understand your rights and options.

Following closely, cases related to deceased estates, known as commissary cases, account for 23.3% of civil business. This underscores the importance of having clear wills and estate plans in place to ensure that your assets are distributed according to your wishes and to minimize potential disputes among beneficiaries.

Personal injury claims make up 8.8% of civil cases. These cases often arise from accidents at work, road traffic incidents, or other situations where individuals have suffered harm due to someone else's negligence. If you've been injured and believe another party is at fault, it's advisable to consult with a solicitor to assess the merits of your claim.

Family law actions represent 8.4% of civil cases, with a significant majority—73%—pertaining to divorce and dissolution proceedings. Navigating the complexities of family law can be emotionally taxing. Whether you're considering separation, dealing with child custody matters, or addressing financial settlements, professional legal guidance can help you make informed decisions during these challenging times.

Understanding these statistics is more than just about numbers; they reflect the real-life issues many people face daily.
