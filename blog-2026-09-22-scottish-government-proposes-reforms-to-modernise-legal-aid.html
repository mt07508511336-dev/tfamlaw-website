Scottish Government Proposes Reforms to Modernise Legal Aid System

22 September 2026

Access to justice is a cornerstone of a fair society, ensuring that everyone, regardless of their financial situation, can understand and exercise their rights. Recognising the need for a more modern and user-friendly legal aid system, the Scottish Government has initiated a consultation on a new Legal Aid (Scotland) Bill. This proposed legislation aims to simplify the current framework, making it more sustainable and focused on the needs of those it serves.

Legal aid provides crucial support to individuals who might otherwise be unable to afford legal representation. It's especially vital in areas like family law and civil disputes, where the outcomes can significantly impact people's lives. The existing system, while comprehensive, has been criticised for its complexity and inefficiencies. The proposed reforms seek to address these issues by streamlining processes and ensuring that resources are allocated effectively.

One of the key aspects of the consultation is to gather input from the public and professionals involved in the legal system. By doing so, the government aims to create a legal aid system that is not only efficient but also responsive to the needs of its users. The consultation period is open until 13 October 2026, providing an opportunity for stakeholders to share their views and contribute to shaping the future of legal aid in Scotland.

At Thompson Family Law, we understand the importance of accessible legal services. We encourage our clients and the wider community to participate in this consultation. Your insights and experiences are invaluable in ensuring that the reformed legal aid system truly serves those who need it most.

For more information on the consultation and how to participate, please visit the Scottish Government's official page: Legal Aid Reform Consultation.
