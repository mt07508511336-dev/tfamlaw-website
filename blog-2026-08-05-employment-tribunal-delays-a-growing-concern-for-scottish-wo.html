Employment Tribunal Delays: A Growing Concern for Scottish Workers

5 August 2026

Recent reports have highlighted a troubling trend in the UK's employment tribunal system: workers are facing unprecedented delays, with some cases not scheduled until 2030. This situation is particularly concerning for employees seeking justice for workplace disputes, as prolonged waits can lead to emotional exhaustion and financial strain.

According to a May 2026 investigation by The Bureau of Investigative Journalism, the backlog in employment tribunals has reached critical levels. Factors contributing to these delays include a shortage of judges, increased caseloads, and insufficient funding. As a result, many workers are left in limbo, unable to resolve issues such as unfair dismissal, wage disputes, or discrimination claims in a timely manner.

In Scotland, while the situation may not be as severe as in other parts of the UK, the ripple effects are still felt. Delays in the tribunal system can deter employees from pursuing legitimate claims, potentially allowing unfair employment practices to go unchallenged. This undermines the fundamental principle of access to justice and can erode trust in the legal system.

For those considering bringing a claim to an employment tribunal, it's essential to be aware of these potential delays. Seeking early legal advice can help in understanding the process and exploring alternative dispute resolution methods, such as mediation, which might offer a quicker resolution.

At Thompson Family Law, we understand the challenges posed by these delays and are committed to supporting our clients through every step of their employment disputes. Our experienced team can provide guidance on the best course of action, ensuring that your rights are protected and that you receive the justice you deserve.

While systemic issues within the tribunal system require governmental intervention, being informed and proactive can make a significant difference. If you're facing an employment dispute, don't hesitate to reach out to us for expert advice tailored to your situation.
