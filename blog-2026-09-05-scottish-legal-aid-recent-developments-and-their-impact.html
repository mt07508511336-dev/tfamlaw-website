Scottish Legal Aid: Recent Developments and Their Impact

5 September 2026

Access to legal aid is a cornerstone of a fair justice system, ensuring that everyone, regardless of financial means, can seek legal redress. Recent developments in Scotland's legal aid landscape have significant implications for individuals and families navigating legal challenges.

In July 2026, the UK Government announced an investment of up to £34 million annually to bolster criminal legal aid. This funding aims to strengthen the legal sector, expedite case processing, and reduce court backlogs. Notably, barristers handling serious sexual offence cases are set to receive fee increases exceeding 13%, acknowledging the complexity of such cases and the need for swift justice for victims. This initiative underscores the government's commitment to building a more robust and sustainable justice system. Source

However, challenges persist. A report from the University College London (UCL) in July 2026 highlighted that the justice system in England and Wales is failing to meet people's needs, with cuts to legal aid contributing to a decline in access to legal representation and support services. This situation leaves many individuals navigating complex legal processes without adequate assistance, potentially undermining both access to justice and confidence in the system. Source

In Scotland, the Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) Act received Royal Assent in May 2026. This landmark legislation enhances protections and support for children and young people, implementing key recommendations from the Independent Care Review. The Act introduces reforms such as remuneration for Panel Chairs, continuity in Children's Hearings, and more flexible interim orders, all aimed at strengthening the Children's Hearings System and reinforcing the principles of child welfare. Source

These developments reflect a dynamic legal aid environment in Scotland. While increased funding and legislative reforms are steps in the right direction, ongoing vigilance is necessary to ensure that legal aid services effectively meet the needs of all individuals, particularly the most vulnerable. At Thompson Family Law, we remain committed to guiding our clients through these changes, ensuring they receive the support and representation they deserve.
