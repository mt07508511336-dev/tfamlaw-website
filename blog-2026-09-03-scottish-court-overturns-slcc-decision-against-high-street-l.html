Scottish Court Overturns SLCC Decision Against High Street Law Firm

3 September 2026

In a significant ruling, the Inner House of the Court of Session has overturned a decision by the Scottish Legal Complaints Commission (SLCC) against a Glasgow high street law firm. This case underscores the vital role that local solicitors play in providing accessible family law services across Scotland.

The case began when a client approached the firm for assistance with a family law matter. The firm, not holding a formal accreditation in family law, accepted the case and later engaged counsel to support the proceedings. The client subsequently filed a complaint with the SLCC, alleging that the firm should have referred her to a specialist from the outset and expressing dissatisfaction with the fees incurred.

The SLCC upheld the complaint, suggesting that the firm's lack of formal accreditation indicated incompetence in handling family law cases. However, upon appeal, the Inner House found this determination to be erroneous. The court highlighted that many high street firms routinely collaborate with counsel to effectively manage complex cases, stating, "Access to justice for consumers throughout Scotland is enhanced by the ability of local High Street firms of solicitors to make use of the expertise of counsel for the benefit of their clients."

This ruling is a positive affirmation for local law firms, emphasizing that formal accreditation is not the sole measure of a solicitor's competence in family law. It acknowledges the established practice of engaging counsel as a means to provide comprehensive legal services, ensuring that clients receive the support they need without unnecessary referrals.
