New Immigration Rule Changes: What Families in Scotland Need to Know

6 August 2026

On 9 July 2026, the UK Home Office introduced significant amendments to the Immigration Rules, known as HC 259, which will take effect on 30 July and 3 August 2026. These changes have important implications for families in Scotland navigating the immigration system.

One notable update is the inclusion of UK-born children as dependants under the Graduate route. This means that individuals on the Graduate visa can now have their UK-born children recognized as dependants, facilitating family unity during their stay in the UK.

Additionally, the amendments aim to align family migration and asylum procedures across various routes. This standardization is intended to simplify the application process, making it more straightforward for families to understand and comply with the requirements.

For families in Scotland, these changes could mean a more streamlined experience when applying for visas or seeking asylum. However, it's crucial to stay informed about the specific details and how they may affect individual circumstances.
