Recent Surge in Legal Aid Immigration Spending: What It Means for Scotland

27 September 2026

Recent data from the Ministry of Justice reveals a significant 40% increase in legal aid spending on immigration cases across the UK, reaching £18.8 million between April and June 2026. This surge is accompanied by a 27% rise in completed legal aid immigration cases, totaling over 19,000 during the same period. A substantial portion of this workload is asylum-related, reflecting the growing demands on the legal aid system.

While these figures pertain to the UK as a whole, they have direct implications for Scotland's legal aid services. The increased demand for immigration-related legal assistance underscores the critical role of legal aid in ensuring access to justice for vulnerable individuals. However, this heightened demand also places additional strain on the system, potentially affecting the availability and quality of legal support in other areas, such as family law and civil disputes.

At Thompson Family Law, we recognize the challenges posed by these developments. Our commitment remains steadfast in providing comprehensive legal support to our clients, ensuring that everyone has access to the justice they deserve. We continue to monitor these trends closely and advocate for the necessary resources to meet the evolving needs of our community.
