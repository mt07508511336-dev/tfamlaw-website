Scottish Legal Aid Fees Increase: What It Means for You

9 September 2026

As of 1 September 2026, legal aid fees in Scotland have increased by 13%, marking a significant step towards enhancing access to justice for individuals who rely on legal aid services. This adjustment reflects the Scottish Government's commitment to ensuring that legal professionals are fairly compensated, thereby encouraging more solicitors to participate in the legal aid system.

For those unfamiliar, legal aid is a system designed to provide financial assistance to individuals who cannot afford legal representation. It covers various areas, including family law, civil disputes, and criminal cases. The recent fee increase is particularly noteworthy for civil Advice & Assistance and Assistance by Way of Representation (ABWOR) services, which have seen an additional 25% uplift to account for previous increments.

So, what does this mean for you? If you're seeking legal assistance and qualify for legal aid, this fee adjustment ensures that more solicitors are available to take on legal aid cases, reducing waiting times and improving the quality of service. For legal professionals, the increase acknowledges the vital work they do and aims to make legal aid work more sustainable.
