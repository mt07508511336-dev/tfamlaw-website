UK Government Proposes Regulation of Funeral Industry to Protect Bereaved Families

8 August 2026

In light of recent events, the UK government has announced plans to introduce formal regulations for the funeral industry. This initiative aims to protect bereaved families from unscrupulous operators and ensure that every individual is treated with the dignity they deserve in death.

The catalyst for this move was the sentencing of Robert Bush, a funeral director from Hull, who received a 20-year prison term for multiple offenses, including fraud and the prevention of lawful burials. Such cases have highlighted the vulnerabilities within the current system and underscored the need for comprehensive oversight.

Under the proposed regulations, funeral service providers will be subject to stringent standards and regular inspections. This framework is designed to prevent misconduct and provide families with confidence in the services they choose during difficult times.

At Thompson Family Law, we understand the profound impact that the loss of a loved one can have. Navigating the legal and emotional complexities that follow can be overwhelming. The introduction of these regulations is a positive step toward ensuring that families receive the respect and support they deserve.

For those who have concerns about funeral arrangements or have experienced issues with service providers, it's important to know that legal avenues are available. Our team is here to offer guidance and support, helping you understand your rights and the options available to you.

As these regulatory changes unfold, we will continue to monitor developments and provide updates to keep our clients informed. Our commitment remains to serve our community with compassion and professionalism, ensuring that you are supported every step of the way.
