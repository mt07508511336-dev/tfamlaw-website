Legal Aid Shortages in Scotland: A Growing Concern for Domestic Abuse Survivors

15 October 2026

Recent reports have highlighted a pressing issue in Scotland: a significant shortage of legal aid is making it increasingly difficult for survivors of domestic abuse to access the legal support they desperately need. This situation not only hampers their ability to escape abusive environments but also poses challenges in securing their rights and safety through the legal system.

Legal aid is a government-funded program designed to ensure that individuals who cannot afford legal representation can still access justice. For many survivors of domestic abuse, legal aid is essential when seeking protective orders, navigating child custody disputes, or addressing financial matters post-separation. However, the current scarcity of legal aid resources means that many are left to face complex legal proceedings without professional assistance.

Campaigners have described the situation as reaching "crisis" levels. The shortage of legal aid solicitors willing or able to take on such cases has resulted in survivors facing prolonged legal battles, often without the necessary support. This not only prolongs their exposure to potential harm but also places immense emotional and financial strain on individuals already in vulnerable positions.

Several factors contribute to this shortage. Financial constraints have made it less viable for solicitors to undertake legal aid work, leading to a decline in the number of practitioners offering these services. Additionally, the complexity and emotional toll of domestic abuse cases can deter solicitors from specializing in this area without adequate support and resources.

Addressing this issue requires a multifaceted approach. Firstly, increasing funding for legal aid would make it more feasible for solicitors to take on these critical cases. Secondly, providing specialized training and support for legal professionals can equip them to handle the unique challenges presented by domestic abuse cases. Lastly, raising public awareness about the availability and importance of legal aid can encourage more survivors to seek the help they need.

At Thompson Family Law, we understand the profound impact that access to legal support can have on the lives of domestic abuse survivors. We are committed to advocating for reforms that ensure everyone, regardless of their financial situation, has the opportunity to seek justice and protection through the legal system. If you or someone you know is facing such challenges, please reach out to us. Together, we can work towards a safer and more just society for all.
