MPs Criticize Government's Response to Legal Aid Crisis

6 November 2026

Recent developments have brought the UK's legal aid system into sharp focus. The Justice Select Committee has expressed deep concern over the government's response to their July 2026 report on access to justice. The committee's initial report highlighted a legal aid system on the brink of collapse, with decreasing availability and rising self-representation leading to an 'unacceptably high risk of miscarriages of justice' and undermining court efficiency.

In their latest statement, the committee criticized the government's response as 'highly disappointing and an inadequate form of response.' They noted that the government failed to clearly indicate which recommendations it accepted or rejected, providing no justification for this approach. This lack of clarity and commitment raises serious questions about the future of legal aid in the UK.

Legal aid is essential for ensuring that everyone, regardless of financial status, has access to justice. The committee's concerns suggest that without urgent reform, the system will continue to decline, leaving many without the legal support they need. This situation is particularly troubling for vulnerable individuals who rely on legal aid for representation in critical matters.

At Thompson Family Law, we understand the importance of accessible legal services. We believe that a robust legal aid system is fundamental to a fair and just society. The government's current stance, as highlighted by the Justice Select Committee, is a missed opportunity to address the growing crisis in access to justice. We urge policymakers to prioritize legal aid reform to ensure that justice is not a privilege for the few but a right for all.

For those seeking legal assistance, it's crucial to stay informed about your rights and the resources available. While the legal aid system faces challenges, support is still available. If you have questions or need guidance, our team at Thompson Family Law is here to help navigate these complexities and provide the support you need.
