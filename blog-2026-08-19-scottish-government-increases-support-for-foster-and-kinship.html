Scottish Government Increases Support for Foster and Kinship Carers

19 August 2026

The Scottish Government has recently announced a 3.8% increase in the Scottish Recommended Allowance (SRA) for foster and kinship carers, set to take effect in April 2026. This adjustment aims to help carers meet the rising costs of living and provide better support for the children and young people in their care.

From April 2026, the weekly allowances will be as follows:

  • 0 to 4 years: £177.68
  • 5 to 15 years: £206.71
  • 16 to 17 years: £283.35

These increases are in line with the Consumer Price Index inflation rate recorded in September 2025. The uplift is part of the Scottish Government's commitment to 'Keep the Promise' to Scotland's care-experienced young people by 2030, supported by an additional £4.3 million investment.

Foster and kinship carers play a vital role in providing safe and loving homes for some of Scotland's most vulnerable children. Ensuring that the financial support they receive keeps pace with the cost of living is essential. This increase in the SRA is a positive step towards acknowledging and supporting the invaluable work these carers do.
