Parental Alienation in Scottish Family Law: Recent Developments and Practical Guidance

27 October 2026

Parental alienation—a situation where one parent manipulates a child to reject the other parent without legitimate justification—has long been a contentious issue in family law. Recent discussions and legal analyses have brought this topic back into focus, highlighting its complexities and the challenges it presents to families and the legal system.

In a recent article by Brodies LLP, the evolving understanding of parental alienation within Scottish family law is examined. The piece underscores the importance of recognizing the nuanced nature of these cases and the necessity for a balanced approach that prioritizes the child's welfare. It also emphasizes the need for legal practitioners to stay informed about developments in this area to effectively advocate for their clients.

At Thompson Family Law, we understand the emotional toll that allegations of parental alienation can take on families. If you find yourself facing such challenges, consider the following practical steps:

  • Seek Professional Guidance: Engage with a family law solicitor experienced in handling parental alienation cases. They can provide tailored advice and represent your interests effectively.
  • Document Interactions: Keep detailed records of all communications and interactions with the other parent and your child. This documentation can be invaluable in legal proceedings.
  • Prioritize Your Child's Well-being: Focus on maintaining a positive and supportive environment for your child. Encourage open communication and reassure them of your unwavering support.
  • Consider Mediation: Explore mediation as a means to resolve conflicts amicably. A neutral third party can facilitate discussions and help both parents reach mutually agreeable solutions.

Parental alienation cases are inherently complex and emotionally charged. It's crucial to approach them with sensitivity and a clear focus on the best interests of the child. At Thompson Family Law, we're committed to guiding you through these challenges with compassion and expertise.
